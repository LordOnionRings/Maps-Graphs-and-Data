Individuals & Societies

Numbers have shapes. Find them.

A spreadsheet full of numbers hides its own story. Turn it into a chart or a map, and the story shows up in about two seconds. That's the whole point of data visualization — and it's why you're about to spend time exploring it.

This isn't a new idea. The Inca recorded numbers using knotted cords called quipu. Ancient astronomers tracked lunar cycles by marking bones and cave walls. Long before spreadsheets existed, people were already turning numbers into something you could see and read.
